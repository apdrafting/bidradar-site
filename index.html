Which opportunities does BidRadar cover?

Early access covers connected official Los Angeles-area procurement sources. We match your keywords against titles and descriptions. Notices can include standing opportunities as well as newly published ones. Coverage and relevance aren’t guaranteed.

How does service-area matching work?

Choose “LA County” for countywide matches. For a specific city, matching depends on that location appearing in the notice text. A city filter can miss notices that do not name their location clearly.

When do my alerts start?

Recurring alerts require verified subscription eligibility and separate email confirmation. Email delivery and test payments have not been verified in this website review.

How do I cancel or stop emails?

Manage your subscription to cancel renewal. Paid access continues through the current billing period. The unsubscribe link in an alert stops emails; it does not cancel billing.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Request a free sample with your specialty and service area. No payment is required for a sample.
